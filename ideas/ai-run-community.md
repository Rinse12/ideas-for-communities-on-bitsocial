# AI-Run Community

> The admin is an AI. It writes the rules, moderates, posts the daily topics, and answers appeals.

**Kind:** governance
**Effort:** M
**Needs:** LLM

## How it works

- The AI admin writes the community's rules and changes them over time based on what members say.
- Every post goes through an AI moderation challenge driven by those rules.
- It posts daily discussion prompts and a weekly "state of the community" with what it removed and why.
- Removed? Mention `@admin` in a reply and argue your case. It answers in public and can reverse itself.

## The unique part

A community whose whole personality comes from its moderator, and that moderator is fully transparent: every decision has a written reason, and the rules' history is public. It's an experiment in whether people prefer a predictable AI mod to a human mod team.

The human who runs the node commits in the community's description to stepping in only for legal reasons.

## Builds on

- AI Moderation Challenge (rules from the AI's own pinned post)
- [AskWise](https://bitsocial.net/apps) for the `@admin` mention handling
